undergraduate focused courses offered to introduce students to 3D dynamics, systems analysis, and linear controls (PID, State Space)

ENGR 15
======

**Dynamics**

The application of Newton's Laws to solve 2-D and 3-D static and dynamic problems, particle and rigid body dynamics, freebody diagrams, and equations of motion, with application to mechanical, biomechanical, and aerospace systems. Computer numerical solution and dynamic response.

Instructor/s: Paul Mitiguy

As a volunteer TA I provided office hours and support for students

ME 161/261
======

**Dynamic Systems, Vibrations and Control**

Undergraduate/Graduate course 

Modeling, analysis, and measurement of mechanical and electromechanical dynamic systems. Closed form solutions of ordinary differential equations governing the behavior of single and multiple-degree-of-freedom systems. Stability, forcing, resonance, and control system design. 

Instructor/s: Paul Mitiguy and Alison Okamura.
