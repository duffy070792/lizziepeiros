Design the Future ran from 2014-2018 during the summer at numerous universities. The programs goal is to show students how they can use their skills to make real impact today through the human centerd design process.

My Role
======

 In 2015 and 2016 I was a mentor/coach working directly with a student team guiding them in the design process. 

 In 2016 I developed the coaches handbook which was used as a resourse for new coaches to learn the design process, have an understanding of their role as a coach, and to instill them with confidence going into the program.

 In 2017 I was part of the Lead Facilitator team adapting the program to include students from the California Academy for the Blind. This was the first year the program was jointly hosted at Berkeley.

 In 2018 I was brought on as a short term contractor to help with the programs execution across 5 states. I developed a lead facilitator training which included learning the instructional material, ensuring appropirate prototyping materials where availible at all locations, and adding additional training and learning materials around safety and people managment.
